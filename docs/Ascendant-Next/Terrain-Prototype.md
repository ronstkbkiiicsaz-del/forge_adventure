# Terrain prototype — what to try

*Double-click **Play Havenbrook** on the Desktop. No command line.*

Walk out of Havenbrook into the wild. You cannot chop or build inside the village.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **WASD** or arrows | Walk |
| **1** | CHOP — trees, rocks, stumps, and your own builds |
| **2** | DIG — pit on the surface, or excavate underground next to an open hole |
| **3** | FLOOR — 1 wood or 1 dirt |
| **4** | WALL — 2 wood or 2 stone |
| **5** | DOOR — 2 wood |
| **6** | TORCH — 1 wood (lights the underground) |
| **7** | LADDER — 2 wood, on a pit |
| **Click** or **hold** the mouse | Use the current tool on the yellow-aimed tile (reach 2) |
| **E** or **Space** | Climb down / up on a ladder |
| **M** | Map |
| **F5** | Save (keeps wood, stone, dirt, pits, and builds) |
| **F9** | Load |
| **Esc** | Quit (or close the map) |

The HUD shows the tool, a hint, wood / stone / dirt, and whether you are on the **surface** or **under**.

## A short loop to feel it

1. Leave town. Press **1**, click a tree. It becomes a stump; you get wood. Click the stump to clear it.
2. Press **2**, click grass. That is a pit (dark hole) and you get dirt.
3. Press **7**, click the pit. Ladder rails show. Stand on it and press **E** — you drop into brown dirt, dark except near the pit.
4. Underground, **2** digs more rooms. **6** places a torch so you can see. **4** places a wall.
5. **E** again climbs back up.
6. **F5** then **F9**: the pit, wall, and materials should still be there.

Townsfolk still walk and talk on the surface. They are hidden while you are underground. The windmill and village art are unchanged.
