# Resources redesign (DRAFT for Steve to react to, 2026-10-13)

*Not final. Wren's proposal so Steve has something concrete to mark up. Replaces the Forge Adventure "nodes pop up
near you" model (scrap and trees spawning around the player) with resources that live in the world.*

## The big change: resources belong to places

- Nothing spawns around the player. Resources are **part of the plane**, generated with it, and stay put until you
  take them (then regrow or are gone, see below). Where you go decides what you find.
- **Surface** = what grows and lies on the ground. **Underground** = what's buried (most mining). **Water** = what
  lives in it. **Sky** = what's up there (later).

## Surface (gather by hand or with a tool)

| Resource | Where | Skill / tool | After taking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logs (oak, willow, yew, ironwood, jungle hardwood, dead wood) | every tree, by biome | Woodcutting / axe tier | stump, regrows over in-game days |
| Plants (herbs, berries, mushrooms, flowers, fibres, sap) | meadows, forest floor, swamp, jungle | Foraging / sickle | regrows |
| Surface stone and boulders | rock clumps, mountain slopes | Quarrying / chisel | some boulders regrow slowly, ridges don't |
| Clay and sand | riverbanks, beaches | Foraging / shovel | regrows |
| Feathers, hide, bone | from creatures you beat or hunt | (drop) | n/a |
| Crops | your own farm plots (fortress) | Farming | replant |

## Underground (most mining)

- The underground layer is **mostly solid dirt and stone** aligned 1:1 with the surface. You dig through it with a
  pickaxe; each tile dug gives a little stone or dirt.
- **Ore and gems are buried veins and pockets** inside it, not floating nodes: you find them by digging toward them.
  What's under you depends on the biome above:

| Above | Common below | Rarer below |
|---|---|---|
| Meadow / plains (white) | iron, coal, clay, limestone | silver, quartz |
| Islands / coast (blue) | salt, coral stone, pearl beds (under water edges) | sapphire, azure crystal |
| Swamp (black) | peat, bog iron, bone deposits | onyx, grave-salt, ancient bones |
| Mountains / volcanoes (red) | iron, copper, sulphur/brimstone, obsidian near volcanoes | mithral, ruby, garnet, dragonfire ash |
| Forest / jungle (green) | amber, root-wood, clay | emerald, jade, living crystal |

- **Depth matters** (when more layers exist): deeper = rarer, harder stone that needs a better pickaxe, more danger.
- **Hard stops:** no digging under deep ocean or rivers; bedrock veins block until a top-tier pick.
- **Finding veins:** a Mining skill perk or a "dowsing" item hints at nearby veins (sparkles in the wall), so it's
  digging with purpose, not random tunnelling.
- **Caves**: natural open caverns underground with exposed veins, creatures and loot; dungeons connect to them.

## Water

- Fishing (rod) in rivers, lakes and sea, by biome: fish, crabs, eels, rare catches; pearls from coast beds.
- Sea encounter zones give water creatures (fights) and their drops.

## Sky (later)

- Feathers and aether materials from sky creatures; "skystone" from floating rocks on some planes (Zendikar!).

## Regrowth and scarcity

- Plants and trees regrow on the seasons calendar (days), boulders slowly, ore never (you dig further instead).
- Each **set plane** has its own resource table (Innistrad: wolfsbane, silver, blessed water; Kaladesh: aether,
  quicksilver; Zendikar: hedron shards, skystone), which is a big reason to travel.

## What happens to today's materials

- The existing material families (logs, ore, ash, sacred stone, waters, dead, plants, scrap, gems, crystal, pearls,
  feathers, hide, brine) map onto the table above; scrap moves to ruins/Kaladesh-style planes; recipes and skills
  stay, with sources changed. Icons we already made carry over.

## Steve's answers (2026-10-13)

1. **Some outcrops on the surface**, but most ore is underground.
2. **Trees regrow over a full season** (SN1 calendar), not a few days.
3. **Fishing is in** ("what good is a game without fishing").
4. **Co-op is partner co-op:** the partner lives in the host's world (same plane saves); they can build their own base
   or live in yours. Resources taken are gone for both.
